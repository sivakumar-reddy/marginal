## Check D. Right censoring

A patient's final admission has no observable follow up. In a dataset with a shared calendar end, those admissions would be censored at that date. MIMIC shifts dates randomly per patient, so there is no shared end and no way to distinguish a patient who was never readmitted from one whose observation window closed.

- Final admissions, one per patient: **223,452** (40.93% of index admissions)
- Non final admissions, follow up observable: 322,528

Of those final admissions, 11,797 ended in death, which resolves the censoring for those patients: they were genuinely not readmitted. The remaining 211,655 are unresolved.

**This is the largest open methodological problem in this domain.** Options are to restrict to non final admissions, which biases toward frequently admitted patients; to treat final admissions as not readmitted, which understates the rate; or to model time to event with censoring, which changes the outcome from binary and does not fit the allocation framework used in the education domain. The choice is made in `12_mimic_cohort.py` and reported with the size of the group it affects.
