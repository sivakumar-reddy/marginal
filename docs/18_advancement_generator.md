## The historical policy is confounded

Officers visited on capacity rating and giving history, which also predict giving without a visit. A naive comparison of visited against not visited therefore attributes officer selection to the visit.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Naive visited minus not visited | +0.2064 |
| True average effect | +0.0437 |
| Confounding bias | +0.1627 |

A shop reading the naive figure would overstate the value of a visit by 372% of its true size. This is generated on purpose. It is the error the domain exists to make visible.

## What this population cannot support

It cannot support any claim about real donors, real conversion rates, or real response to contact. The four group structure is asserted, not measured. The parameters are placeholders. Any public reference to this domain is framed as simulation.
