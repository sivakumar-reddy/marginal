## What this bounds

The gap between predicted mean and base rate in the leave one module out table is the calibration failure that matters most. A model trained on six modules and applied to a seventh with a very different withdrawal rate will be miscalibrated in level even if it ranks well. Ranking survives transfer better than calibration does, and the allocation step needs both: ranking to choose who, calibration to estimate how many outcomes are averted.

Any claim about day 0 performance in public material must cite the leave one module out figure, not the pooled cross validated figure, because the pooled figure describes a condition no deploying institution is in.
