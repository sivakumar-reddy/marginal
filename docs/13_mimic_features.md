At admission is the only point at which the stay itself could still be changed. At discharge the information is better and the only remaining lever is follow up. That is the same trade the education domain found between reach and information, in a sharper form.

## Diagnosis grouping

ICD-10 codes only. ICD-9 coded admissions are dropped from the comorbidity features and retain a count of zero, which understates history for older admissions. This is a real limitation and is not corrected here.

Groupings are by ICD chapter prefix. A validated comorbidity index such as Elixhauser or Charlson would be better. A homemade score presented as though it were validated would be worse than saying so.

## Features

| Feature | Available at | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| gender, anchor_age | both | Static patient attributes |
| admission_type, admission_location | both | How the patient arrived |
| insurance, marital_status, race | both | Recorded at registration |
| service_grouped | both | Clinical service, small services pooled as OTHER |
| prior_admissions | both | Count of earlier admissions for this patient |
| prior_los_total | both | Total days spent in hospital across earlier admissions |
| prior_n_diagnoses | both | Codes recorded across earlier admissions |
| prior_circulatory and seven others | both | Earlier admissions with a code in that chapter |
| los_days | discharge only | Length of this stay |
| discharge_location | discharge only | Where the patient went |
| cur_n_diagnoses, cur_circulatory and seven others | discharge only | Codes for THIS admission |

The separation of `prior_` from `cur_` is the leakage boundary. Codes for the current admission are assigned during and after the stay from documentation produced across it. Using them at the at_admission point would let the model see the stay it is predicting from its start. The script asserts their absence rather than relying on the column list being correct by inspection.

## Limitations

ICD-9 coded admissions contribute no comorbidity signal and carry zeros. Older admissions therefore look artificially healthy. The size of this group is not yet quantified and should be before any comorbidity result is reported.

Comorbidity groups are ICD chapter prefixes, not a validated index. They will underperform Elixhauser or Charlson and should not be described as either.

`race`, `insurance` and `marital_status` are included as predictors. They carry real signal about readmission risk and they also encode access to care rather than clinical need. Their inclusion is a choice, and the distributional consequences of allocating on a model that uses them belong in the distribution analysis for this domain.
