## Check B. Is anyone left to help at each decision point?

At a decision point on day D, a student is in the risk set only if they have not already unregistered. Students who left before D cannot be affected by an intervention at D and are removed. The table below shows what is left.

| Decision point | Day | Already gone | Still in risk set | Withdraw later | Later rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| week_4 | 28 | 5,040 | 27,553 | 5,032 | 18.26% |
| week_8 | 56 | 6,061 | 26,532 | 4,011 | 15.12% |
| week_12 | 84 | 6,860 | 25,733 | 3,212 | 12.48% |

5,040 registrations unregister before day 28, which is 49.6% of all withdrawals. Those students are outside the reach of any intervention timed at week 4 or later. This is a ceiling on what allocation at these decision points can achieve, and it belongs in the limitations before any result is reported.
