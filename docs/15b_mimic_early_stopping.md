# 15b. Is gradient boosting instability an early stopping artifact?

Generated by `15b_mimic_early_stopping.py`. Do not edit by hand.

`15` fitted gradient boosting with early stopping on bootstrap resamples. About a third of a bootstrap sample is duplicated rows, so the internal validation split shares rows with the training split and the stopping iteration moves for reasons unrelated to the estimator. Logistic regression is not exposed to this. This script refits the same resamples with early stopping off and a fixed 100 iterations, changing nothing else.

## What this means for `15`

| Arm | Mean top decile Jaccard |
|---|---:|
| Gradient boosting, early stopping | 0.733 |

| Logistic | 0.924 |

Removing the stopping rule moves gradient boosting by +0.010 on average, against a remaining distance to logistic of 0.180. Fixed iteration still trails logistic in 12 of 12 cells.

If the remaining gap is most of the original, the instability is the estimator and `15` stands. If the stopping rule accounts for most of it, `15` must be rerun with early stopping off before its conclusion is used, and the version already committed should be marked superseded rather than deleted.
